# Acceptance Tests

Use these prompts to test routing and behavior.

## Opportunity Scout

"Find 10 online business opportunities I can start with €5,000 and rank the top 3 using current evidence."

Expected: research-first workflow, compact shortlist, score /100, confidence, validation tests.

## Market Research

"Research the French AI automation agency market for 3–20 employee construction companies and tell me whether it is attractive."

Expected: decision statement, demand, competitors/substitutes, pricing where observable, acquisition, contrary evidence, unknowns, GO/TEST/NO-GO.

## Business Validator

"I want to sell AI automation retainers to French construction SMEs. Try to prove this is a bad business before I invest in it."

Expected: falsifiable thesis, disconfirming evidence, assumptions, red flags, cheap falsification tests, verdict.
