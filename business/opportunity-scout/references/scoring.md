# Opportunity Scoring Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to its maximum. Use evidence, not vibes.

| Dimension | Max |
|---|---:|
| Pain severity + frequency | 15 |
| Willingness to pay / existing spend | 12 |
| Demand / growth signal | 10 |
| Customer accessibility | 10 |
| Gross-margin potential | 8 |
| Recurrence / retention | 8 |
| Speed to first revenue | 8 |
| Differentiation / defensibility | 7 |
| Capital efficiency | 7 |
| Execution simplicity | 5 |
| Founder fit | 5 |
| Regulatory / platform resilience | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Confidence modifier

Do not mechanically alter the score. Add an evidence-confidence label:

- **High** — multiple recent, credible sources and observable market behavior.
- **Medium** — credible evidence exists but key economics or demand assumptions remain unverified.
- **Low** — thesis rests mainly on anecdotes, proxies, or speculative assumptions.

## Decision bands

- **80–100:** Strong candidate; validate immediately.
- **65–79:** Promising; investigate key uncertainties before building.
- **50–64:** Weak unless founder advantage is unusually strong.
- **<50:** Drop by default.

A high score with low confidence is not a GO. It is a research priority.
