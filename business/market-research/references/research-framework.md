# Market Research Framework

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Primary official data, company filings, regulator data, industry associations.
2. Direct market behavior: prices, job postings, product listings, customer reviews, public procurement, ad activity, traffic/search proxies where credible.
3. High-quality industry research and reputable journalism.
4. Customer interviews, forums, Reddit, communities, support threads, reviews.
5. Expert opinion and founder intuition.

Do not present levels 4–5 as statistical proof.

## Market sizing

Use bottom-up sizing whenever feasible:

`reachable customers × realistic annual spend = serviceable revenue pool`

If using top-down TAM/SAM/SOM:

- cite the source year;
- explain category boundaries;
- avoid fake precision;
- distinguish market revenue from the revenue realistically accessible to a new entrant.

## Competitor analysis

For each meaningful competitor or substitute capture:

- target customer;
- core promise;
- pricing / pricing model if observable;
- acquisition channel clues;
- strengths;
- weaknesses / complaints;
- differentiation opportunity.

## Demand evidence

Strong signals include repeated spend, urgent search behavior, active procurement, established budgets, recurring manual work, high-cost mistakes, compliance obligations, and customers already assembling workarounds.
