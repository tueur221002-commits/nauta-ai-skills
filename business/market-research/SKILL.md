# Market Research

Research only what is decision-relevant.

## Start with the decision

State the decision the research must support, for example:

- enter this market or not;
- choose between two niches;
- pick a customer segment;
- choose pricing;
- select an acquisition channel;
- decide whether a pain is valuable enough to solve.

Then identify the 3–7 uncertainties most likely to change that decision.

## Research workflow

1. Define category, geography, target customer, time horizon, and business model.
2. Research customers and jobs-to-be-done.
3. Gather direct and proxy demand signals.
4. Map competitors and substitutes, including doing nothing or manual work.
5. Collect observable pricing and packaging where available.
6. Assess distribution and customer-acquisition channels.
7. Estimate market size only when useful; show method and assumptions.
8. Identify structural trends, regulation, platform dependencies, and technological changes.
9. Summarize what evidence supports and contradicts the thesis.
10. State what remains unknown and the fastest way to learn it.

Use `references/research-framework.md` for the evidence hierarchy and market-sizing rules.

## Output format

- Decision to make
- Executive conclusion
- Key evidence
- Customer / problem
- Demand signals
- Competitor / substitute map
- Pricing / monetization evidence
- Acquisition / distribution
- Market size, if decision-relevant
- Risks and contrary evidence
- Unknowns
- Recommendation: GO / TEST / NO-GO
- Next validation action

Keep facts, calculations, estimates, and assumptions visibly distinct.
