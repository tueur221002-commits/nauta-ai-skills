# Offer Scorecard

Score each offer hypothesis out of 100. Score evidence confidence separately.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Pain severity / economic consequence | 15 |
| Frequency / recurrence | 10 |
| Buyer awareness and urgency | 10 |
| Measurability of outcome | 10 |
| Demonstrated willingness to spend / prior attempts | 10 |
| Speed to visible value | 10 |
| Low behavior-change requirement | 8 |
| Fit with existing tools/workflow | 7 |
| Repeatability across ICP | 8 |
| Implementation simplicity | 5 |
| Gross-margin potential | 4 |
| Data / compliance / platform resilience | 3 |

## Bands
- 80-100: strong offer candidate; run a real paid test.
- 65-79: promising; resolve major unknowns before scaling.
- 50-64: weak or under-evidenced; redesign or research.
- Below 50: reject in current form.

## Evidence confidence
- High: repeated behavioral/economic evidence across comparable customers plus real spending/attempts.
- Medium: repeated reported pain with partial quantitative evidence.
- Low: mostly opinions, hypotheticals, inference, or one-off anecdotes.

A high score with low evidence confidence is not validation.

## Comparison rule
When comparing variants, prefer the offer with the strongest combination of economic pain, measurable result, fast time-to-value, low adoption friction, and repeatability—not the offer with the most features.
