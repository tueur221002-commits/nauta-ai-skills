# Business Red-Team Checklist

## Customer
- Is the pain frequent, expensive, urgent, or mandatory?
- Who owns the budget?
- Is the buyer the same as the user?
- What happens if the customer does nothing?

## Competition
- Are incumbents already bundling the feature/service?
- Is the market crowded because demand is real, or because entry is easy and margins are poor?
- What switching costs protect incumbents?

## Acquisition
- Can the target buyer be identified and reached economically?
- Is there a credible initial channel?
- Does the proposed price support the likely CAC and sales effort?

## Economics
- What is the gross margin after human delivery, software, refunds, support, and payment fees?
- Is recurring revenue actually recurring, or merely described that way?
- How many customers are needed for the target monthly revenue?
- Is fulfillment capacity a hidden ceiling?

## Risk
- Platform dependency?
- Regulation/privacy/compliance?
- Supplier concentration?
- AI commoditization?
- Customer concentration?
- Working-capital burden?

## Falsification tests
Prefer tests that generate behavior rather than opinions:

- paid pilot;
- deposit/preorder;
- outbound offer to a defined list;
- landing page + qualified traffic;
- concierge/manual MVP;
- pricing test;
- customer interview with evidence of existing spend/workaround.
